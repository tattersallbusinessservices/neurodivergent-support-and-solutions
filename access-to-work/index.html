Access to Work Support

Funding and support to help you access employment or self-employment.

Access to Work is a government scheme that provides funding and support to help people with disabilities and health conditions access employment or self-employment. For neurodivergent individuals, it can fund support workers, equipment, training, and other adjustments needed to work effectively.

Access to Work is a government scheme, there's no cost to apply.

The Access to Work process: we assess your specific needs and how Access to Work might help, guide you through completing forms and gathering evidence, and support you through the submission process, liaising with Access to Work on your behalf.

Help managing your Access to Work funding and adjusting support as your needs change.
