Founded by a doctor with lived experience of autism and ADHD, we truly understand neurodivergent experiences and challenges.

Our Services

  • Coaching & Support — one-to-one coaching for executive function, ADHD management, autism support, and family guidance.
  • Reports & Advocacy — professional reports written by a qualified doctor, supporting EHCP, workplace adjustments, and occupational health.
  • Access to Work Support — guidance through the Access to Work application process.
  • Services for Employers — guidance on supporting neurodivergent employees and implementing reasonable adjustments.
