About Neurodivergent Support and Solutions

Founded by a doctor with lived experience of autism and ADHD, we truly understand neurodivergent experiences and challenges.

As a neurodivergent person myself, I understand the daily realities you face. This isn't theoretical for me, it's personal. This lived experience informs every aspect of how I work with clients.

This gap inspired me to create Neurodivergent Support and Solutions. I wanted to build a service that combines my medical expertise with my lived experience, one that truly understands what it means to be neurodivergent in a neurotypical world, and that celebrates neurodivergent ways of being.

Our mission: to empower neurodivergent individuals to understand themselves, celebrate their strengths, and thrive in a world not always designed for them. By combining lived experience with professional expertise, we provide practical, compassionate, and affirming support that helps people build fulfilling lives on their own terms.
