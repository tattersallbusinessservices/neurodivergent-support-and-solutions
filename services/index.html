Our Services

Comprehensive support tailored to your unique needs and circumstances.

Coaching & Support: one-to-one coaching for executive function, ADHD management, autism support, and family guidance.

Reports & Advocacy: professional reports written by a qualified doctor with expertise in neurodivergence, supporting EHCP applications, workplace adjustments, and occupational health purposes.

Access to Work Support: guidance through the Access to Work application process, including completing forms and gathering evidence.

Services for Employers: guidance on supporting neurodivergent employees, creating inclusive policies, and implementing reasonable adjustments.

Many clients benefit from combining services, for example coaching alongside a workplace assessment, or self-guided tools with periodic check-ins.
