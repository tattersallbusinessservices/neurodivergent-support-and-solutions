Our Services

Comprehensive support tailored to your unique needs and circumstances.

Coaching & Support

One-to-one coaching for executive function, ADHD management, autism support, and family guidance.

Reports & Advocacy

Professional reports written by a qualified doctor with expertise in neurodivergence, supporting EHCP applications, workplace adjustments, and occupational health purposes.

Access to Work Support

Guidance through the Access to Work application process, including completing forms and gathering evidence.

Services for Employers

Guidance on supporting neurodivergent employees, creating inclusive policies, and implementing reasonable adjustments.

Many clients benefit from combining services, for example coaching alongside a workplace assessment, or self-guided tools with periodic check-ins.
